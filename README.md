# AI and Mathematics: a transparent viewpoint map

This repository maps published viewpoints on artificial intelligence in mathematics and mathematics education. It is a discussion aid, not a ranking of people or a forecast of AI capability.

Each source receives three provisional, reviewable scores from 0 to 100:

- **Outlook**: anxious (0) to hopeful (100)
- **Evidence basis**: speculative (0) to data-supported (100)
- **Source reliability**: lower (0) to higher (100), based on expertise, transparency, method, primary sourcing, and relevance

Scores refer to a source's argument, not the worth, prestige, or intelligence of its author. Every entry must link to a specific work and explain its score in plain language.

## Current materials

- [Source ledger](data/source-ledger.csv): works included and their scope.
- [Assessments](data/assessments.csv): current scores and reasons.
- [Methodology](docs/METHODOLOGY.md): rubric, limits, and review protocol.
- [Prompt and review record](prompts/INITIAL-REVIEW-RECORD.md): the initiating prompt, the model-assisted assessment record, and corrections.
- [How to contribute](CONTRIBUTING.md): propose sources, authors, scores, or better evidence.

## What this project needs

We welcome additions that represent different fields, levels of teaching, countries, and views. In particular, contributors should flag weak evidence, duplicate reporting, mistaken characterizations, and important omitted authors.
